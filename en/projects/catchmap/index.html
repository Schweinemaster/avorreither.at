CatchMap

A fishing app for Austria that puts your catches and spots on a map. Fully local, and it works offline.

CatchMap is a fishing app for Austria. It shows your catches and your spots on a map. It is my own project, it is in development, and I plan to release it on the Play Store.

The app runs fully on the phone and is built offline-first. There are no accounts, no server and no sync, so your data stays with you: privacy and security by design. When you switch phones, you take your data along with export and import.

It is built with Flutter and Dart, with drift as the local database and Mapbox for the map. I taught myself Flutter and Dart while building it.
